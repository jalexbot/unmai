**The claim:** Tamil Nadu's unemployment rate is the highest in India and has worsened since DMK took office in May 2021.

**What the data shows:**

According to the Centre for Monitoring Indian Economy (CMIE), Tamil Nadu's unemployment rate as of Q2 2024 stood at approximately 4.2%. The national average for the same period was 7.8%.

States with higher unemployment rates than Tamil Nadu (Q2 2024, CMIE):
- Haryana: 11.2%
- Rajasthan: 9.1%
- Jharkhand: 8.4%
- Bihar: 8.1%
- Delhi: 7.2%

Tamil Nadu ranked among the lower half of states by unemployment rate — not the highest.

**Trend analysis:**
The PLFS 2022-23 data (published by MoSPI, Government of India) shows Tamil Nadu's Usual Status unemployment rate at 3.8%, below the national average of 5.1%.

**Verdict: Myth.** The available official and independent data consistently place Tamil Nadu's unemployment rate below the national average and well below the states with the highest unemployment in India. The claim is not supported by data.

**Note on data limitations:**
Unemployment measurement methodology varies between CMIE (monthly, household survey) and PLFS (annual, government survey). Both sources point in the same direction for Tamil Nadu.
