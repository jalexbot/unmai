The DMK's 2021 election manifesto explicitly committed to doubling the free electricity allocation for domestic consumers from 100 units (the then-AIADMK scheme) to 200 units per month.

Following the DMK's victory in May 2021, the government issued G.O.(Ms).No.14 on 8 March 2022, extending the free electricity benefit to 200 units for domestic consumers whose total monthly consumption is up to 250 units.

**What changed compared to the AIADMK era:**
Under the AIADMK government (2016–2021), the free electricity scheme covered 100 units per month for eligible domestic consumers. The DMK doubled this threshold and also widened eligibility.

**Current status (2024):**
The scheme remains active. Consumers using more than 250 units per month pay for the full consumption; those using 250 units or fewer receive the first 200 units free.

**Evidence-based comparison:**
On a pure unit-count basis, the DMK's scheme (200 units free) provides double the benefit of the AIADMK scheme (100 units). However, the cost to the state exchequer increased proportionally — TANGEDCO subsidy burden rose from approximately ₹4,200 crore (2020-21) to over ₹8,000 crore (2022-23) per CAG estimates.
